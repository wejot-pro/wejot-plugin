# Item Quality Checklist

Use this checklist while drafting each module and during final review.

## Comprehension

- Are subject, timeframe, object, and requested judgment explicit?
- Are unfamiliar terms defined in respondent language?
- Is the stem short enough to understand without rereading?
- Does it ask one construct rather than two?

## Retrieval

- Can respondents realistically remember or access the answer?
- Is the recall period specific and appropriate to event frequency?
- Does the item avoid requiring unavailable records or excessive mental arithmetic?

## Judgment

- Does the wording avoid leading, prestige, blame, or assumed behavior?
- Are sensitive questions justified and positioned appropriately?
- Is “not applicable,” “do not know,” or “prefer not to answer” available when substantively valid?

## Response mapping

- Are options distinguishable, sufficiently exhaustive, and consistently ordered?
- Are scale direction, endpoints, and units visible?
- For multiple choice, are minimum and maximum selections justified?
- Can the submitted value be mapped unambiguously to the intended indicator?

## Final traceability

Record the objective or indicator for every item. If an item has no use in analysis or decision-making, remove it. If an objective has no supporting item, revise the blueprint before generation.
